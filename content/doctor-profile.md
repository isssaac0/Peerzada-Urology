Dr. Peerzada Faisal Masood is a fellowship-trained urologist specializing in endourology, uro-oncology, andrology, reconstructive urology, and renal transplant surgery, with particular expertise in minimally invasive laparoscopic and robotic techniques.

He completed his MBBS and MS in General Surgery & Renal Transplant, followed by an M.Ch in Urology from PGIMER Dr. RML Hospital, and further specialized training in Minimal Invasive Urology — Laparoscopic and Robotic Surgery — at AIIMS, Delhi.

Throughout his training, Dr. Masood has been recognized with multiple national and international awards, including gold medals for academic and surgical excellence and travel fellowships from leading urological societies. His approach combines this depth of technical training with a clear, honest, and reassuring style of communication — helping patients and families understand every step of their care.
