title: Laparoscopic Pyeloplasty in a case of a crossing vesse
category: Laparoscopy + Robotic Surgery
description: >-
  Laparoscopic pyeloplasty with a crossing vessel is technically more demanding
  than a straightforward UPJ obstruction. The main challenge is safely
  dissecting the vessel and choosing the correct reconstruction. During
  dissection, avoid excessive traction, thermal injury, or inadvertent vessel
  injury, which can cause significant bleeding or compromise renal perfusion.

  The new uretero-pelvic junction must be tension-free, dependent, and well vascularized.

  The reconstructed ureter should generally lie anterior to the crossing vessel when a dismembered transposition repair is indicated

  In this case, a crossing vessel was present, which was supplying the lower pole (only functional parenchyma), was identified intraoperatively and safely preserved
