title: Long segment PUJO
category: Reconstructive Urology
description: >-
  Unlike standard PUJ obstructions, which typically involve a very short,
  localized segment, a "long segment" stricture spans down the upper ureter.
  This makes standard surgical fixes more challenging.

  In such cases, one may not be able to perform a dismembered pyeloplasty; hence, there should always be a backup before you proceed with transection. This can be in the form of a spiral flap (Culp-DeWeerd), a vertical flap (Scardino-Prince), Buccal Mucosa Graft (BMG) ureteroplasty, or ureterocalicostomy. 

  This patient was taken for laparoscopic pyeloplasty. A preoperative retrograde pyelogram was performed because the CT findings were equivocal and showed a long-segment PUJ stricture. The case was converted to open, and luckily, we were able to do A-H dismembered pyeloplasty after good renal mobilisation.

  In most cases, we do not do RGP's but If non-invasive imaging does not clearly map the stricture, RGP provides real-time visualization of the pelvic-ureteric geometry.
