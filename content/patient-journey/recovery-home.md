Recovery is gradual, and it looks a little different for everyone depending on the procedure. The key points and interactive tools on this page are here to help you understand general patterns of what's expected — they're a starting reference, not a substitute for speaking with your care team about anything that concerns you.

### Physical recovery vs. everyday routine

It's worth separating two different timelines that often get blurred together: how quickly the surgical site itself heals, and how quickly you feel ready to resume your normal routine. The first is largely dictated by the procedure and your body's own healing pace — this is what the recovery milestones below track. The second is more personal, and it's completely normal for energy levels and confidence to lag a little behind physical healing, especially in the first couple of weeks. Both matter, and both are worth being patient with.

### When to actually call your care team

A general rule that helps: if something feels like a clear change from how you were doing the day before — new pain, new fever, new difficulty — it's worth a call rather than waiting to see if it resolves on its own. The tools below are built around exactly this kind of pattern recognition, but they're a starting point for the conversation, not a replacement for it.
