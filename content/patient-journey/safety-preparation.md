The period just before surgery is focused on safety — making sure everything is confirmed and in place so the procedure itself goes smoothly. The key points below summarize what this involves; a bit more detail follows.

### The anesthesia consultation

This is one of the more important conversations in this phase. The anesthesia team will review your health history, current medications, and the specific procedure planned, then walk you through exactly what to expect — from the type of anesthesia used to how you'll likely feel as you wake up afterward. It's a good moment to raise any concerns, especially if you've had a difficult experience with anesthesia in the past.

### Fasting and final instructions

Fasting guidelines exist for genuine safety reasons — an empty stomach reduces the risk of complications during anesthesia. Instructions are tailored to your surgery time and are usually provided in writing so there's no ambiguity about when to stop eating and drinking. Following them precisely, even if they feel inconvenient, is one of the simplest ways to keep the day itself running smoothly.

This is also a good time to ask any last questions — a clear, calm start to surgery day makes a real difference.
