problemDiagnosisBody: Kidney and ureteric stones are among the most common

Endourology covers the full range of scope-based, minimally invasive treatments for stones and other conditions within the urinary tract. Using fine endoscopes and laser technology, these procedures reach the kidney and ureter through the body's natural passages or a small keyhole access point — avoiding the need for large incisions altogether, and often letting patients return to daily life far sooner than older surgical approaches would allow.

Stone disease can range from a small, incidentally-found stone that simply needs monitoring, to a large, complex stone burden requiring a carefully staged treatment plan. Every case is assessed individually — imaging is reviewed in detail, and the technique recommended (RIRS, PCNL, or ureteroscopy) is matched to the stone's specific characteristics rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Dr. Peerzada Faisal Masood's training in minimally invasive urology means these procedures are approached with an emphasis on complete stone clearance in as few procedures as possible, careful preservation of kidney function throughout, and a recovery designed to get patients back to their lives quickly — with practical, individualized guidance afterward to help prevent stones from coming back.
