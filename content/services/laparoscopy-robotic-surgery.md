Laparoscopic and robotic-assisted surgery aren't tied to one specific condition — they're the minimally invasive approach applied across many procedures in this practice, from endourology to uro-oncology to reconstructive and transplant surgery, whenever they're the right fit for the case.

The appeal of these techniques isn't the technology for its own sake — it's what smaller incisions and greater surgical precision actually mean for a patient: less pain, less blood loss, a shorter hospital stay, and a faster return to normal life, without compromising on how thoroughly the underlying problem is treated.

Dr. Peerzada Faisal Masood trained specifically in Minimal Invasive Urology — Laparoscopic and Robotic Surgery — at AIIMS, Delhi, and applies these techniques to reduce surgical trauma and support faster recovery wherever they are clinically appropriate, while always being upfront about the cases where an open approach genuinely remains the better choice.
