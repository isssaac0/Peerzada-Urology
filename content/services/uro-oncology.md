Uro-Oncology addresses cancers affecting the kidney, bladder, prostate, and broader urinary tract. This is one of the most technically demanding areas of urology, requiring a careful balance between completely removing the cancer and preserving as much healthy function as possible — a balance that shapes every decision from diagnosis through recovery.

No two cancer diagnoses are quite the same, even within the same organ. Stage, grade, location, and a patient's overall health all shape what the right treatment plan looks like, which is why staging and pre-operative planning are treated as seriously as the surgery itself — getting this right is what makes precise, function-preserving surgery possible in the first place.

Dr. Peerzada Faisal Masood's training in minimally invasive and robotic surgical techniques supports this balance — aiming for thorough cancer control alongside a recovery process that respects each patient's quality of life, with honest, clear communication at every stage about what to expect.
