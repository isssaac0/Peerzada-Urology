Reconstructive urology involves surgical repair of structural problems within the urinary tract — often technically demanding procedures that require careful, precise reconstruction using the body's own tissue to restore normal function, sometimes years after an original injury or complication first occurred.

These aren't one-size-fits-all repairs. The right reconstructive technique depends on exactly where the problem is, how it developed, and what tissue is available to work with — which is why careful diagnostic mapping before surgery is treated as just as important as the operation itself.

Dr. Peerzada Faisal Masood brings his training in minimally invasive and robotic-assisted surgery to this work, aiming for durable, function-restoring repairs with as little trauma to the patient as the specific reconstruction allows, and clear, honest guidance throughout recovery about what to expect.
