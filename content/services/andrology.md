Andrology focuses on male reproductive and urological health — an area that patients sometimes hesitate to seek care for, but which responds well to timely, accurate diagnosis and treatment. It's often the delay itself, more than the underlying condition, that makes these concerns feel bigger than they need to be.

Concerns in this area can range widely — from fertility difficulties, to hormonal imbalances, to structural conditions that developed gradually and were never mentioned to a doctor. Getting a clear, accurate diagnosis is usually the biggest step, since it turns an open-ended worry into a specific, treatable problem.

Dr. Peerzada Faisal Masood approaches these consultations with a direct, respectful, and unhurried style, aiming to identify the true underlying cause of a concern and to explain treatment options clearly before proceeding — with full confidentiality and without judgment, at every step.
